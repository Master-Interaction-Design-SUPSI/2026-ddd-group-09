---
title: "Camouflage Against Facial Recognition: Material Strategies to Interfere with AI Surveillance"

authors: Elena Nobis - Giulia Giussani 
---
This project explores *visual camouflage* techniques designed to interfere with AI-based facial recognition systems. Within the theme of *Materialities of AI*, it looks at how surveillance is physically embedded in cameras, faces and bodies, and how makeup, hairstyles, garments and accessories can disrupt it. Camouflage is read as a form of {#116964|visual resistance} to technological surveillance. [[our lens|This is our perspective on the phenomenon.|type=note;color=green]]

What techniques are used to avoid recognition by AI cameras, and what do they use?

We built a dataset, [data_group_9](LINK-AL-DATASET-PUBBLICATO), of 123 examples from 35 sources: research (69), projects (35) and news (19). Key references: [[Harvey, A. CV Dazzle|https://adam.harvey.studio/cvdazzle|type=bibliography]]; [[Adv-Makeup, IJCAI 2021|https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2021/0173.pdf|type=bibliography]]; [[NII Privacy Visor|https://www.nii.ac.jp/userimg/press_20121212e.pdf|type=bibliography]]; [[Reflectacles|https://www.reflectacles.com/|type=bibliography]].

We grouped the examples into *seven families. {crimson|Dazzle makeup} [[cv dazzle look]] and **occlusion* (masks, glasses, hair) dominate, together making up {#116964|more than half of the examples}, while adversarial patterns, infrared and reflective materials and thermal fabrics are less frequent. Most strategies exploit the same weakness: AI reads faces through contrast, symmetry and visible features. See the [[dazzle club protest]] video.
