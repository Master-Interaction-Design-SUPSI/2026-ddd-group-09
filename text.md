---
title: Camouflage Against Facial Recognition: Material Strategies to Interfere with AI Surveillance

authors: Elena Nobis - Giulia Giussani 
---
This project explores *visual camouflage* techniques designed to interfere with AI-based facial recognition systems. Within the theme of *Materialities of AI, it looks at how surveillance is physically embedded in cameras, faces and bodies, and how material interventions such as makeup, hairstyles, garments and accessories can disrupt it. Camouflage is read as a form of **visual resistance* to technological surveillance.

Which camouflage strategies against facial recognition emerge from the sources we collected, and how can they be grouped?

We built a dataset (data_group_9) of 123 examples from 35 sources: research (69), projects (35) and news (19). Key references: [[Harvey, A. CV Dazzle|https://adam.harvey.studio/cvdazzle|type=bibliography]]; [[Adv-Makeup, IJCAI 2021|https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2021/0173.pdf|type=bibliography]]; [[NII Privacy Visor|https://www.nii.ac.jp/userimg/press_20121212e.pdf|type=bibliography]]; [[Reflectacles|https://www.reflectacles.com/|type=bibliography]].

We grouped the examples into *seven families* of camouflage. *Dazzle makeup* and *occlusion* (masks, glasses, hair) dominate, together making up {#116964|more than half of the examples}, while adversarial patterns, infrared and reflective materials and thermal fabrics are less frequent. Most strategies exploit the same weakness: AI reads faces through contrast, symmetry and visible features, which material interventions can disrupt.
